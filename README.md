# The Cost of Unconscious Choices

A short assessment for business owners, entrepreneurs and middle managers, made for Chantelle Botha ([Phoenixconfidence.com](https://phoenixconfidence.com)). Ten questions on a 1 to 5 slider show how small unconscious habits affect effectiveness in life and leadership, with a result in one of three bands and a link to book a call with Chantelle.

**Live:** https://brucewade15.github.io/the-cost-of-unconscious-choices/

One self contained `index.html`. Nothing the person types is saved or sent anywhere.
